# Getting your Kevin DMs to me without the Slack integration

Three options, best first.

## Option 1 — Copy/paste as text (BEST: fastest, cheapest, searchable)
1. Open the DM with Kevin in the Slack **desktop app**.
2. Scroll up to the start of your internship, then let it load back down.
3. Click on the first message, scroll to the bottom, **shift-click** the last message (or just Cmd+A inside the message pane).
4. Cmd+C.
5. Paste into a plain text file and save it here as:
   `Slack/kevin-dm-01.txt`
6. Slack only holds so much in the DOM at once, so it'll probably take 3–6 passes going down the history.
   Name them `kevin-dm-01.txt`, `kevin-dm-02.txt`, ... in chronological order (oldest = 01).

Text is the best format: I can read all of it, quote it accurately, and pull the real phrasing of
your questions straight out of it.

## Option 2 — Print to PDF
1. Open the DM at **app.slack.com** in Chrome (the browser version, not the app).
2. Cmd+P → Destination: **Save as PDF**.
3. Save as `Slack/kevin-dm.pdf`.
Catch: it usually only captures what's currently loaded, so scroll up first and repeat in chunks
(`kevin-dm-part1.pdf`, `kevin-dm-part2.pdf`, ...). I can read PDFs.

## Option 3 — Screenshots (works, just heavier)
Drop them in `Slack/` named in scroll order: `kevin-01.png`, `kevin-02.png`, ...
I can read images fine — this is slower for me and I can't search it, but if it's what's easy for
you right now, do this and don't overthink it.

## What I'm looking for in there
- The moments you were genuinely lost, in your own words
- Kevin's phrasing you didn't understand at first (great week-1 material)
- Tasks handed to you, so the week-by-week timeline is factually right
- Any wins, praise, or "oh that's actually good" moments
- Running jokes between you two

## What I will NOT put on the site without asking
Anything sensitive: client names, salaries, candidate details, internal strategy, anything Kevin
said that he wouldn't want published. I'll flag anything borderline and you decide.
